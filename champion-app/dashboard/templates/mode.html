Read this before you switch — paper first

The champion is proven only in replay — historical data run through the same code. It has never traded a live account. The paper trial is not just the designed path, it is enforced: live mode cannot be enabled until the app's audit log shows a completed paper trial of 90 days, including at least one completed quarterly selection cycle, with no unresolved reconciliation mismatch. Run the app in PAPER mode against your free Alpaca paper account, read the daily reports, and only then decide about real money.

Live results are expected to be lower than the replay record. The program's own planning haircut is roughly 18–23% a year if the edge holds at all, against 24–31% in replay — and the edge may not hold. In LIVE mode the app places real orders in your own Alpaca account, with your money, at your own risk. You can switch back to paper at any time, and STOP ALL halts order placement immediately — but stopping does not sell anything you already hold.

Stop all

STOP ALL halts the engine immediately: it will place no further orders in any mode until you resume it here, and it cancels every resting order at the broker, showing the result order by order. It does not sell or liquidate anything — positions you already hold stay in your Alpaca account, untouched, for you to manage at the broker. Use it whenever anything looks wrong.

{{STOP_BLOCK}}

Protections — identical in live mode

The adopted v9 protections apply unchanged with real money: a plain −15% floor from each episode's starting balance, no profit ladder; a first halt liquidates the pot, sits out 90 days, and restarts once under a fresh −15% floor; a second halt ends the run. There is no Pot 2 in any mode. Hard caps apply in both modes too: five positions at most, 20 percent of the pot in any one name, and the pot size you set under this app's shipped $10,000 ceiling.

One limit matters more with real money: the app can only act while it is running. If a floor is breached while the machine is off, the sale happens at the next startup, at whatever price is then available. The floor is enforced when the app is awake; it is not a resting broker order.
